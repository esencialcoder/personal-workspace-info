# Privacy Policy

## Purpose

Personal Workspace Utility is a private application intended solely for use by its owner.

## Information access

The application accesses only the account information and services explicitly authorized by the owner.

## Use of information

Authorized information is used exclusively to perform actions requested by the owner and to support personal productivity workflows.

## Storage and security

Authentication credentials and application data are stored in private systems controlled by the owner. Reasonable technical measures are used to restrict access and protect stored information.

## Sharing

Information is not sold, rented, shared for advertising, or made available to unauthorized third parties.

## Retention

Information is retained only for as long as required for the application's personal operation or until the owner removes it.

## Revocation and deletion

The owner may revoke access or delete stored application information at any time.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.
